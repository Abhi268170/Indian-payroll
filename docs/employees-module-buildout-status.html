Add Employee Wizard
Fully Built
The 4-step guided form used when hiring a new person. Like an onboarding checklist.
  • ✅ Step 1 — Name, job title, department, join date, location
  • ✅ Step 2 — Annual salary, how it's split (Basic, HRA, etc.)
  • ✅ Step 3 — Date of birth, PAN, father's name, address
  • ✅ Step 4 — Bank account for salary transfer
  • ✅ Can create new departments/designations mid-wizard
  • ⚠️ Can't add extra salary components during the wizard
  • ⚠️ IFSC code entered but not verified against bank records
  • ❌ No warning shown when email ID cannot be changed later

Where each person's salary gets transferred. Must be accurate — wrong details = unpaid employee.

Previous Employer Salary History
Not Built
When someone joins mid-year (e.g. September), they already earned salary at another company. This info is needed to calculate the correct income tax for the rest of the year.

⚠️ What Goes Wrong Without These Features

Real-world impact of launching without the missing pieces.

🔴 If we launch today without fixing these…

  • → Mid-year joiners get wrong income tax deducted — prior employer salary not captured, TDS calculated as if they earned zero before joining.
  • → No way to offboard employees — resigned staff stay "Active" in the system forever; their salary might keep getting processed.
  • → Wrong money transferred to wrong banks — IFSC is never verified, so a typo can send salary to a non-existent or wrong branch.
  • → Customers can't migrate from existing payroll systems — no bulk import means manually entering 100+ employees one by one; a deal-breaker for most companies.
  • → Salary raise for any employee requires re-entering everything — no quick "change CTC" screen; user must redo the entire 4-step wizard.
🗓️ Suggested Build Order

What to tackle first, based on how much it would hurt customers if missing.

# What to Build Why It Can't Wait Priority
1 Previous Employer Salary (YTD)
Form on employee profile for prior-company income & tax		 Without this, income tax is wrong for anyone who joined mid-year. Legal/compliance risk. Must Have

Auto-fill bank name from IFSC code, show verified badge		 Prevents salary going to wrong bank. Builds trust with payroll managers. Must Have
3 Salary Raise Screen
Simple "change CTC" page — not the full 4-step wizard		 Every company does annual raises. Current flow forces re-entering all details — terrible UX. Must Have
4 Employee Exit Flow
Resign/terminate form, last working day, status change		 Staff turnover is constant. Without this, exited employees keep showing as active. Must Have
5 Bulk Import (Spreadsheet Upload)
CSV upload for employees, salary, prior history		 No customer with 50+ employees will onboard without this. Sales blocker. High
6 More Columns & Filters on List Page
UAN, PAN, onboarding status, column selector		 HR teams live in this list. Limited columns make it hard to spot issues at a glance. High
7 Tax Declaration Form
New-regime investment declaration per employee per FY		 Needed before year-end tax season. Can be deferred to Q3. Medium
8 Salary Revision History
Timeline of past CTCs on the salary tab		 Auditors and employees both ask for this. Nice-to-have for launch. Medium
